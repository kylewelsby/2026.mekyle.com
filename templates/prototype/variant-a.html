Summary

ViaCara helps people find a therapist, counsellor, coach or hypnotherapist who fits what they are going through. Clients share what they need. Practitioners share who they are and how they work. Then ViaCara introduces them.

Join the practitioner waitlist Hiring? Read the open proposal

Context

My partner is an independent therapist. Her diary stayed full while others around her waited for clients. That told me good practitioners are being asked to be marketers.

Decision

Leave an acting Principal role and build the introduction service myself, with what I learnt building platforms where correctness, regulation and cost matter.

Consequences

  • Opening in Manchester and Stockport first, then across the UK.
  • No subscription. Practitioners pay for an introduction only when a client books.
  • The first two introductions are free.
