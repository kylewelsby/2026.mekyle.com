## Who runs this site

You can reach us by email at [kyle.welsworth@pm.me](mailto:kyle.welsworth@pm.me).

## What we collect

This is a static personal site. There are no accounts, no forms and no tracking cookies, so we hold very little.

**Analytics.** We use Plausible, a privacy-friendly analytics service hosted in the European Union. It sets no cookies and collects no personal data. It records only aggregated, anonymised figures such as page views and referring sites, which cannot be traced back to you. The [Plausible data policy](https://plausible.io/data-policy) explains this in full.

**Hosting.** The site is served by Cloudflare Pages. As with any web host, Cloudflare processes technical request data such as IP addresses in order to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse.

**Email.** If you write to us, we keep your message and contact details for as long as we need them to reply and to keep a reasonable record of the conversation.

## Our lawful basis

We rely on our legitimate interests: serving and securing the website, understanding aggregate usage so we can improve it and replying to people who contact us. We do not sell your data and we do not share it for advertising.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it and object to how we use it. To make a request, email [kyle.welsworth@pm.me](mailto:kyle.welsworth@pm.me).

If you are not happy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the page and revise the date at the top.
